# Research Brief: Instagram Content Squad (Agência Cognitiva)

## 1. Executive Copywriting (B2B SaaS/Consultancy)
- **Target**: C-Level, Directors, and Managers.
- **Key Strategy**: Value-over-features. Executives want to know how IA reduces cost, increases scale, and ensures governance.
- **Frameworks**:
  - **AIDA**: Attention (hook) -> Interest (data/problem) -> Desire (solution/vision) -> Action (CTA).
  - **PAS**: Problem (Shadow AI/Failure) -> Agitation (Risk/Cost) -> Solution (Agência Cognitiva).
  - **4 Cs**: Clear, Concise, Convincing, Credible.
- **Anti-Patterns**: Being too technical/nerdy without business context; "PowerPoint talk" (vague promises); generic AI hype.

## 2. AI Trends & Executive Curation (2024-2025)
- **Top Trends**: Agentic AI (autonomous agents), AI Governance/LGPD, Democratization (Low-code), and "Evolution Loop" (Observability).
- **Curation Role**: Translating "The model has 1T parameters" into "This agent can process 1M events for your triage pipeline without human error."

## 3. Premium Tech Visual Design (2025)
- **Estilo**: "Advanced Minimalism". Clean lines but with depth.
- **Elements**: 3D humanoid robots (friendly but professional), high-contrast gradients (Dark Blue/Neon), and experimental typography for authority.
- **Reference**: The provided Drive folder samples (Dark blue, serif titles, 3D assets).

## 4. Contrast Marketing Framework
- **The Hook**: Start with a "useful information" (News/Trend) -> Contrast with "Traditional/Common Market Approach" -> Pivot to "Agência Cognitiva Product/Methodology".
- **Logic**: "The market sells you a project (static). We build a product (living/autonomous)."
