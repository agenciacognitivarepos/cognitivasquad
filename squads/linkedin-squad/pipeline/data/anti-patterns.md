# Anti-Patterns: LinkedIn Brand Protection

## 1. Never Do
- **The "Broetry" Cliche**: Using one-sentence paragraphs for every single line. It looks artificial and signals low-quality "growth hacking".
- **Generic AI Fluff**: Using terms like "Revolutionize", "Game-changer", or "Deep dive into the future" without specific technical substance.
- **Engagement Pod Bait**: Asking for "Claps if you agree" or "Comment 'YES'". It results in low-quality leads and algorithm penalties.
- **The Body Link**: Putting an external URL in the first 100 words of the post. It kills the post distribution immediately.

## 2. Always Do
- **Native Formatting**: Use bullet points and white space to make technical content digestible on mobile.
- **Evidence-Based Hooks**: Start with a specific number, a direct benefit, or a bold (but documented) market shift.
- **Human Response**: Reply to every comment with a question to keep the conversation depth high.
- **Agência Cognitiva Context**: Always connect the technical insight to a real-world service (AI Discovery, Pilot Agent, Governance).
