# Domain Framework: LinkedIn Content Engine

## Phase 1: Strategic Research
1. Identify high-momentum "Signals" (Market news, tech launches, industry reports).
2. Rank stories based on relevance to Agência Cognitiva's core business (Agentic AI, Governance, Scalability).
3. Select ONE story to develop in-depth.

## Phase 2: Angle Identification
1. Generate 5 distinct angles from the selected story:
   - **Fear**: The risk of ignoring this trend.
   - **Opportunity**: The competitive advantage.
   - **Educational**: A deep dive into the "How".
   - **Contrarian**: A challenging take on the hype.
   - **Inspirational**: The future vision.
2. Select the angle that best aligns with the current marketing objective.

## Phase 3: Copywriting Composition
1. **The Hook**: First 2-3 lines optimized for mobile "See More" click.
2. **The Meat**: Value-first breakdown using PAS or BAB frameworks.
3. **The Bridge**: Seamlessly connect the trend/news to a specific service or product from Agência Cognitiva.
4. **The CTA**: Specific, low-friction next step (Question, invitation to profile, etc.).

## Phase 4: Quality Control & Review
1. Score against specificity, authority, and dwell time.
2. Ensure technical accuracy and brand-aligned tone.
3. Optimize formatting (white space, bullet points).
