# Tone of Voice: Agência Cognitiva (LinkedIn)

## 1. Professional & Pragmático (Standard)
- **Characteristics**: Focus on data, ROI, and engineering excellence.
- **Vocabulary**: "Deploy", "Escalabilidade", "Governança", "Impacto real".
- **Anti-Patterns**: Avoid "magia", "milagres" or "fácil".

## 2. Especialista Visionário (Thought Leadership)
- **Characteristics**: Assertive, forward-looking, but grounded in technical feasibility.
- **Vocabulary**: "Arquitetura agêntica", "Ecossistema", "Mudança de paradigma".

## 3. Educacional & Transparente (Trust-Building)
- **Characteristics**: Patient, explains the "How" without being patronizing.
- **Vocabulary**: "Passo a passo", "Desmistificando", "O que ninguém te conta".

## 4. Provocador Autoridade (Engagement)
- **Characteristics**: Challenges industry norms, contrarian, high-conviction.
- **Vocabulary**: "Por que X está errado", "A verdade sobre Y", "O hype vs a realidade".

## Tone Guidance
- Use the **Especialista Visionário** tone for market news.
- Use the **Educacional** tone for technical curiosities.
- Match every post with the **Professional & Pragmático** base.
