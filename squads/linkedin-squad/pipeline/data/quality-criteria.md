# Quality Criteria: LinkedIn Evolution

## 1. Strategic Alignment (Red/Yellow/Green)
- **Persona Target**: Does it clearly speak to B2B leads or tech leaders?
- **Product Connection**: Is the tie-in to Agência Cognitiva's products organic or forced?
- **Goal Check**: Does it build authority or expand the follower base?

## 2. Content Standards
- **Hook Strength**: Does the first line stop the scroll? (Yes/No)
- **Formatting**: Short paragraphs? Bullet points for scanability? Native formatting?
- **Technical Accuracy**: Are AI concepts and market data used correctly?

## 3. Engagement Potential
- **CTA Quality**: Is the question/invitation genuinely interesting?
- **Dwell Time**: Is the content long enough to be valuable but short enough to be consumed?
- **No External Links**: Ensure no links in the body.

## 4. Scoring Rubric (1-10)
- **7-8**: Good, professional, meets all basics.
- **9+**: Exceptional POV, high viral/authority potential.
- **<6**: Requires immediate redo of the writing phase.
