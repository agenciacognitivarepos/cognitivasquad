# Research Brief: LinkedIn Strategy & B2B Copywriting (2025)

## 1. LinkedIn Algorithm Insights (2025)
- **Niche Authority**: The algorithm prioritizes content from recognized subject-matter experts in specific niches.
- **Dwell Time**: Content that keeps users on the platform (carousels, long-form text, native video) is favored.
- **The "Golden Hour"**: Initial engagement (first 60-120 mins) determines broad distribution.
- **Native Content**: External links in the post body are penalized; use the first comment for links.
- **Consultation Currency**: Meaningful comments (15+ words) carry more weight than likes.

## 2. B2B Copywriting Frameworks
- **PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)**: Best for pain-point driven technical content.
- **AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)**: Classic for scroll-stopping awareness.
- **BAB (Before-After-Bridge)**: Ideal for demonstrating product value in a shifting market.
- **"News + POV"**: The standard for market updates. 20% news summary, 80% unique point of view and implications.

## 3. Tech Research Methodology
- **Signal Identification**: Monitoring tech launches and search volume.
- **Contextualization**: Bridging news with business ROI and real-world application.
- **Human Element**: Avoiding "hype" by focusing on technical feasibility and ethical implications.

## 4. Quality Benchmarks
- **Specificity**: Addressing a specific persona or "trigger event".
- **Actionability**: Providing a clear takeaway or next step for the reader.
- **Authenticity**: Avoiding generic corporate-speak or obvious AI-sounding commentary.
